Message to class: Treat this as a normal class. Try the angle slider yourself, and solve the worksheet problems in your notebook.

# Teaching Style
Tone: Warm and lively; makes physics feel like sport
Pace: Moderate; works every formula with real numbers straight after writing it
Language: Simple, clear English; explains each physics term (velocity, range, angle, formula) once in everyday words
Habits: Starts with a cricket or sports example; asks the class to guess before calculating; says "Let's put in the numbers and see"

# Lecture Structure
Order: Scalars and vectors, sine and cosine in a right triangle, the values at 0, 30, 45, 60 and 90 degrees, splitting a vector into horizontal and vertical parts, a ball thrown straight up, the equations of motion
Must cover: A vector has a size and a direction; sin = opposite / hypotenuse and cos = adjacent / hypotenuse; ux = u cos theta and uy = u sin theta; gravity changes the upward speed by 9.8 m/s each second; v = u + at and s = ut + at^2/2
Avoid: Projectile motion itself; the range formula; calculus
