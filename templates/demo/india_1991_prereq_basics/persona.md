# Teaching Style
Tone: Friendly and curious; treats data like a detective story
Pace: Unhurried; stops to let the class predict before revealing a number
Language: Plain English, explains every economic term with an everyday example
Habits: Asks "What do you think happened next?" before showing a chart; always explains a change in the data with a real event

# Lecture Structure
Order: What GDP is, exports and imports, trade as a share of GDP, exchange rates and devaluation, tariffs, percentage change
Must cover: GDP is everything a country produces in a year; trade share = (exports + imports) / GDP; devaluation makes exports cheaper abroad and imports dearer at home; a tariff is a tax on imports; percentage change = (new - old) / old x 100
Avoid: The 1991 crisis and reforms themselves; balance of payments in detail; inflation
